Web-ROTI

Web-ROTI (Return On Time Invested) is a tool to gauge the quality of meetings and improve them.

It is super easy to get started

  1. Create a new meeting
  2. Let everyone vote by passing a phone around, through the direct link or the QR code
  3. Check out the result

ROTI can easily be measured on a whiteboard or paper, but you run the risk of getting a biased result when the voters see other votes. In Web-ROTI every vote is given without bias, and as a bonus it calculates the average as well.

Originally created at Diversify by Kristoffer Richardsson, Björn Ekengren, Daniel Valfridsson, Uzi Landsmann, Keijo Kurkinen, Anders Möllmark and Christer Sandberg.
